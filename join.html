02 · Is HERO right for you?

You do not need to arrive as a finished researcher.

Previous research experience and publications are welcome, but they are not required. What matters more is curiosity, motivation, reliability, and a genuine interest in the questions we study.

Programming experience is useful for some projects, but many studies also involve interviews, surveys, qualitative analysis, statistical analysis, literature reviews, education research, or community engagement.

01

Be curious

Ask thoughtful questions and be willing to explore problems without obvious answers.

02

Be reliable

Communicate clearly, stay organized, and follow through on commitments.

03

Be collaborative

Research improves when people exchange ideas, feedback, and perspectives openly.

04

Care about evidence

Good research requires careful methods, ethical decisions, and attention to data quality.

03 · How we work

Mentoring for independence.

Our goal is not simply to assign tasks. Students grow into researchers who can shape questions, evaluate evidence, and take ownership of their work.

↗

Increasing independence

Early projects receive more guidance. As your experience grows, so does your responsibility for research decisions.

↔

Collaborative research

Students are encouraged to help shape research questions, methods, interpretations, and future directions.

✓

Constructive feedback

Good research involves iteration. Giving and receiving thoughtful feedback is part of the process.

◎

Meaningful impact

We connect research questions to real experiences of developers, students, educators, and communities.
